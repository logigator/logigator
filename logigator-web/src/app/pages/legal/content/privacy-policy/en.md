## Running the website

Logigator runs on its own servers in Austria. Database backups are also kept in Austria.

**Cloudflare.** Every request to logigator.com passes through Cloudflare, Inc. (USA), which protects the website from attacks and delivers it quickly. Cloudflare processes your IP address and the technical details of the request.

**Server logs.** For every request, our servers log the IP address, the time, the address requested, the browser and the result. We need these logs to find faults and detect abuse. They are deleted after one month.

**Abuse protection.** To slow down repeated sign-in attempts and mass registrations, the server counts requests per IP address. This counter expires after an hour at most.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and reliable operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Your account

For an account we store your username, your email address and your password, the password only as a hash that cannot be reversed. If you sign in with Google, we store the ID of your Google account instead of a password. You can choose to add a profile picture, a description, a website and up to three links to social networks.

After you register, we send you an email to confirm your address. Accounts whose address is not confirmed within three months are deleted.

While you are signed in, your browser keeps a session cookie. The session ends 30 days after you last used it, or when you sign out.

You can delete your account at any time in the account settings. This also deletes all your circuits and stars. The data leaves the backups when they are overwritten after three months.

The legal basis is the performance of the terms of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

## Circuits and community

Circuits you save to the cloud are stored on our servers, together with their name, description and preview image. You decide who can see them:

- **Only you**: nobody but you.
- **Anyone with the link**: whoever has the link can view the circuit. It appears in no listing and in no search engine.
- **Everyone**: the circuit appears in the community, is visible to everyone and is indexed by search engines.

Your public profile shows your username, your profile picture, your profile details, how long you have been a member, your public circuits and the public circuits you have starred. On a public circuit, anyone can see who has starred it. When someone copies a circuit, the copy links to the original.

The legal basis is the performance of the terms of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The data is kept until you delete it or your account.

## Cookies and browser storage

Without your consent, we only store what the website and the editor need to work (§ 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021):

- `preferences`: your language and colour scheme, one year.
- `consent`: your cookie choice, one year.
- `lg_sid` and `isAuthenticated`: your sign-in, up to 30 days.
- In the browser's local storage: the editor's settings and projects saved in the browser. This data does not leave your browser unless you upload a project to the cloud.

Anything else is only used with your consent. You can change or withdraw it at any time from "Cookie settings" in the website's footer or the editor's Help menu.

## Statistics with PostHog

If you consent, we use PostHog to understand how Logigator is used and to find faults. The provider is PostHog, Inc. (USA). The data is stored in the EU (PostHog Cloud EU, Frankfurt).

PostHog records:

- which pages you open and, on the website, what you click,
- which features of the editor you use,
- recordings of your sessions in the editor, meaning what happens on the editor's screen, including the drawing board; text you type into fields is hidden,
- error messages with technical details,
- load times, browser, operating system, screen size and the language of the interface.

The data is tied to a random ID kept in a cookie (`ph_…_posthog`) and in the browser's local storage. We do not link it to your account. An approximate location (country and region) is derived from your IP address; the IP address itself is not stored.

Events are deleted after one year, recordings after 30 days. PostHog is not loaded without your consent; if you withdraw it, no further data is recorded.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021).

## Bug reports

You can report a bug from the editor. A report is only sent when you click "Send report". It contains your description, the error message with technical details, information about your browser, operating system, graphics and screen, the address you were on, the editor's recent log lines, the open project and, if you are signed in, the ID of your account.

The report is delivered to us by email and noted in the server log. We delete it once the bug is fixed, and after six months at the latest.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in fixing bugs (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Email

Emails about your account, such as confirming your address or resetting your password, are sent through SMTP2GO (New Zealand). If you email us, the message is stored with Zoho Corporation B.V. (Netherlands) in the EU. Bug reports arrive there as well.

The legal basis is the performance of the terms of use, or answering your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

## Signing in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Logigator sends you to Google Ireland Limited. Google passes us the ID of your Google account, your email address, your name and your profile picture. We use the name as a suggestion for your username and copy the profile picture onto our server. Google learns that you are signing in to Logigator.

The legal basis is the performance of the terms of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

## Video on the home page

The explainer video on the home page comes from YouTube (Google Ireland Limited). It only loads when you click it; before that, YouTube receives no data. After the click, your browser sends your IP address and technical details to YouTube, and YouTube may store data in your browser.

The legal basis is the consent you give by clicking (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021).

## Your rights

You can object to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time; processing that took place before remains lawful.

To exercise these rights, write to [business@logigator.com](mailto:business@logigator.com).
